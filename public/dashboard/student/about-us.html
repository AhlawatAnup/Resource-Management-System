AI Data Center Resource Management

The AI Data Center Resource Management platform is designed to make access to computing resources across the university easier and more efficient. It provides a central platform for students, teachers, and administrators to request, manage, and use lab machines, cloud instances, and software in a streamlined way.

The platform simplifies resource sharing by providing a clear dashboard, automated notifications, and a simple workflow for submitting and tracking requests.

Feedback and suggestions are welcome as the platform continues to evolve to meet the needs of its users.
