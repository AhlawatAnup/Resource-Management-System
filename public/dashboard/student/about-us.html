About U.I.E.T Resource Sharing Center

The U.I.E.T Resource Sharing Center is designed to make access to computing resources across the university easier and more efficient. It provides a central platform for students, teachers, and administrators to request, manage, and use lab machines, cloud instances, and software in a streamlined way.

The platform simplifies resource sharing by providing a clear dashboard, automated notifications, and a simple workflow for submitting and tracking requests. It helps users know which resources are available, see upcoming reservations, and stay informed about important updates.

Built with a responsive design, the system works well on both desktop and mobile devices. It aims to reduce delays, improve transparency, and make collaboration smoother within the university community.

This initiative was developed by the Department of Computer Science and Engineering to improve lab utilization and support students and faculty in their academic work. Feedback and suggestions are welcome as the platform continues to evolve to meet the needs of its users.
